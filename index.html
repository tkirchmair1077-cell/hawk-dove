Weights (40/20/20/10/10), camp cut-offs (±20), alert thresholds (15 / 25 points) and the 30-day half-life are proposed model parameters and are not validated. Scores with low coverage are renormalised over the observed dimensions only; a high score with low coverage is not a high-confidence reading.

  • Dimension codes come from two scoring passes on the same official quotes and are not independently validated.
  • Several current stances sit at exactly +50.0, which reflects one dominant event; read those as a weak signal.
  • A current-stance camp is printed only when there is a published stance and an agreed preferred-path observation within 90 days. That camp is not a structural classification.
  • Structural lean is published only with at least four agreed events and at least 365 days between the first and the last. Where those tests fail, the camp is insufficient evidence — it is not guessed.
  • Two events remain unresolved and are excluded from calculations: Schmid 15 November 2026 and Barkin 9 November 2026.
  • Every scored quote on this page is verbatim from an official Board or Reserve Bank page, official PDF, or FOMC statement. Licensed-press wording is not used here.
  • This is not an investment recommendation and is not validated for investment decisions.

Camps

Structural classification from the 12–18 month window (20 May 2025 to 20 November 2026): Hawk if structural lean > +20, Centrist if −20 to +20, Dove if < −20. Cut-offs are not validated. People who do not meet the evidence rule are listed separately, with the reason — no camp is assigned by guesswork.

Alerts

Materiality rules from the specification, not statistical significance. Thresholds are not validated. Camp changes and counter-lean alerts require analyst sign-off. A current-stance move can raise a watch or material-shift flag even when structural lean is still insufficient evidence.

Panel A — Structural lean

Primary axis: structural lean from −100 (dove) to +100 (hawk), with unvalidated ±20 camp bands. Each bubble is an official with a published structural lean. Bubble size is the number of agreed, official, policy-relevant evidence items — not a market-influence weight. A diamond on the lower rail of each lane marks current stance (90-day, 30-day half-life) and is joined to the bubble by a thin line. 2026 voters sit on the upper lane with a solid ring; alternates and non-voters on the lower lane with a dashed ring. Officials without a published structural lean are listed below the axis, not plotted at a guessed x-value.
