Weights (40/20/20/10/10), camp cut-offs (±20), alert thresholds (15 / 25 points) and the 30-day half-life are proposed model parameters and are not validated. Scores with low coverage are renormalised over the observed dimensions only; a high score with low coverage is not a high-confidence reading.

  • Seven events are pending analyst review and have no published final score (the two scoring passes are not averaged).
  • Structural lean is not computable yet: the specification requires 12–18 months of evidence across different data regimes, which were not fetched in this run. Camps on this page refer to current stance, not a structural classification.
  • “Not found” means no official transcript was retrieved in this pass. It does not prove that remarks were never made.
  • Every scored quote on this page is verbatim from an official Board or Reserve Bank page or PDF. Licensed-press wording is not used here.
  • This is not an investment recommendation and is not validated for investment decisions.
